How It Works

Simple by design, powerful when it matters

Offline Learning

Lessons and questions are cached on the device so practice continues even without internet.

Adaptive Practice

Local adaptive scoring adjusts difficulty as the learner answers, keeping practice meaningful.

Regional Languages

Practice and instructions are available in English, Kannada and Hindi.

Low-Data Sync

Tiny progress packets sync only when connectivity returns, keeping data usage minimal.
